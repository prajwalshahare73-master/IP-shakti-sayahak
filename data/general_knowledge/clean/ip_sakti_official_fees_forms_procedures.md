Important Notes on Fees
All fees below are government statutory fees. Professional/agent fees are NOT included.
Fees are time■sensitive. Always verify on the official IP India portal or relevant official notification before
filing.
Applicant categories (Patents/Designs): 'Reduced' = Natural person / Startup / Small entity / Educational
institution (with Form 28 where required). 'Large' = Other applicants.
Applicant categories (Trademarks): 'Reduced' = Individual / Startup / Small Enterprise; 'Others' = Companies,
LLPs, etc.
GI fees reflect the GI (Amendment) Rules 2025 (notified 3 Nov 2025), which reduced several fees
substantially.

7. Important Application / Filing Procedures (High■Level)
7.1 Patent Filing & Examination (India)
1) Prepare specification (Form 2) and filing data (Form 1). 2) File e■application on ipindia.gov.in (Patents). 3)
Pay filing fee (Schedule 1). 4) Application publishes at 18 months (or earlier on Form 9). 5) File Form 18 for
examination within prescribed time. 6) Respond to examination report; attend hearing if needed. 7) On
acceptance, pay grant & publication fees (if applicable) and receive certificate. 8) Pay renewals from 3rd year
onwards.
7.2 Trademark Filing & Examination (India)
1) Conduct trademark search (tmrsearch.ipindia.gov.in). 2) File Form TM■A (e■filing) with class(es) and fee.
3) Examination report issued; respond to objections. 4) On acceptance, mark is published in Trade Marks
Journal. 5) 4■month opposition window. 6) If no opposition/successful defence, registration certificate issued.
7) Renew every 10 years (Form TM■R).
7.3 GI Filing (India)
1) Define geographical area and product characteristics. 2) Prepare GI application (GI■1) with link between
quality/reputation and origin. 3) File with GI Registry. 4) Examination → acceptance → publication. 5)
Opposition window. 6) Registration; add authorised users (GI■3). 7) Renew every 10 years (GI■2).
7.4 Copyright Registration (India)
1) Create account on copyright.gov.in. 2) File Form XIV with work details and soft copy. 3) Pay fee as per
category. 4) 30■day objection window. 5) Examination. 6) Receive registration certificate.
7.5 Design Registration (India)
1) Prepare representations of design (views). 2) File Form 1 (e■filing) with fee. 3) Examination →
acceptance/refusal. 4) Registration certificate. 5) Renew after 10 years (up to 5 more) via Form 3.
