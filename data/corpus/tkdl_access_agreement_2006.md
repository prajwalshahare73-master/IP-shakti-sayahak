Traditional Knowledge Digital Library (TKDL)
Access Agreement
General Information:
1.TKDL is proprietary database of Government of India (Related Departments
are : Controller General of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks, National Institute
of Science Communication And Information Resources, Council of Scientific
and Industrial Research, Department of Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani,
Siddha and Homoeopathy).
2.TKDL is five international languages (English, French, German, Spanish and
Japanese) database, which converts Indian Traditional Knowledge (Ayurveda,
Unani, Siddha, Yoga, etc.) from existing prior art available in Hindi, Sanskrit,
Arabic, Persian, Urdu, etc. to five international languages.
3.TKDL is a bridge between Patent Examiner and prior art (available through
published books in Ayurveda and published books in Unani).
Obligations of Providers:
1.National Institute of Science Communication And Information Resources
(NISCAIR), Council of Scientific and Industrial Research, is the implementation
agency of the project.
2.NISCAIR shall ensure uninterrupted access to signatories of the agreement on
24x7 basis.
3.NISCAIR shall render necessary training support to TKDL users as and when
needed.
4.NISCAIR shall render assistance through TKDL in search and examinations as
and when needed by the respective patent offices.
