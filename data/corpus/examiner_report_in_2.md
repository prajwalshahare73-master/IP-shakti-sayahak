Claim 1 is not clear with respect to expression, 240-60% of Belgiri (Aegle marmelos Corr), Zanjabeel (Zingiber officinale Roxb.) and Teewaj (Holarrhena anti

dysentrica Wall.)?. These refer to name of the plants. Claims are not clear regarding which plant part or its extract is being used in the claimed composition. The %
age should be given in weight or volume. Claim 7 is not clear in respect of expression “such as".

Claim 9 is not sufficiently definitive. This claim attempt to define the subject- matter in terms of the result to be achieved, such as ?
effective against diarrhaca & dysentery substantially as herein described?, without providing the technical features necessary for
achieving these results. The technical features required to achieve the effect should be the essential part of the claims. A claim
should be definitive and understandable without reference to the description.

Claims refer to a composition and process of making the composition comprising

Belgiri (Aeg! marmelos Corr.),
Zanjabeel (Zingiber officinale Roxb.) and

‘Teewaj (Holarrhena anti dydentrica Wall.)

composition claim. Hence claims are not allowable under section 3(e).

present application claims a composition effective against diarrhaca and dysentery, where as there is no supporting clinical
data, mentioned in the specification. The specification only discloses and describes the composition. The invention can?t be carried *
out without undue experimentation. Hence the main claim lacks support and the application lacks disclosure.
